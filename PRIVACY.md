# Perfect Prime Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Perfect Prime is a browser extension developed by JonLuca DeCaro. It automates selected Prime Video interface actions, such as skipping intros, recaps, or ads and playing the next episode.

## Data the extension processes

Perfect Prime examines the Prime Video page interface locally to find the controls needed for the features the user enabled. It does not collect or transmit video-viewing history, account information, page content, form data, credentials, or communications.

## Settings storage

The extension stores only its feature and display preferences in the browser's extension sync storage. Depending on the browser and the user's browser-account settings, the browser provider may synchronize those preferences between the user's devices. The developer does not receive or have access to the synchronized data.

## Collection and sharing

Perfect Prime makes no external network requests of its own and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It uses no analytics or advertising services.

## Retention and user control

Preferences remain in browser-managed extension storage until the user changes them, clears extension data, or removes the extension, subject to the browser provider's sync behavior.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document and its updated date. Questions may be sent to [chrome@jonlu.ca](mailto:chrome@jonlu.ca).
